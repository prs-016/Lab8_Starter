### How are graceful degradation and service workers related? 

Graceful degradation and service workers are related, as service workers allow websites and web apps to degrade gracefully where there is no Internet connection. In other words, service workers enable graceful degradation in web development by deciding whether to serve cached resources or fetch new ones from the Internet. Without service workers, losing the network would cause the entire site or app to break.
